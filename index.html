First, choose your IIgs and load its ROMs

The IIgs can't switch on without the ROM chips Apple built into it. They are still under copyright, so they aren't included here: choose or drop your own ROM image files. They stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere. The browser remembers them, so you only need to do this once.

  • Apple IIgs ROM 3 Required

    The toolbox, Applesoft BASIC, the Monitor and the built-in firmware: 256 KB (262,144 bytes), chips 341-0737 and 341-0748 together.

    Often named apple2gs.rom or APPLE2GS.ROM3

  • Apple IIgs ROM 01 Required

    The same for ROM 01: 128 KB (131,072 bytes), chip 342-0077-B.

    Often named apple2gs_rom01.rom or 342-0077-b

  • Character ROM Required

    The characters of the text screens, with MouseText, chip 341-0170. 4 KB (4,096 bytes).

    Often named apple2gs.chr or 341-0170-a.chr

Pick several files at once, or a .zip containing them (a whole ROM collection works too). Dumps with their banks in another order are put right.

Release to load these ROMs

3.5" drive 1 No disk has saved games
3.5" drive 2 No disk has saved games
5.25" drive 1 No disk has saved games
5.25" drive 2 No disk has saved games
Hard disk No disk has saved changes

Drop disk images anywhere on the page: 800K ones (.po, .2mg) go in a 3.5" drive, 5.25" ones (.dsk, .do, .po, .nib, .woz) in a 5.25" drive, and hard disks of up to 32 MB (.hdv, .po, .2mg) in slot 7, or drop onto a drive's row. Several at once, such as all the disks of a set, give each drive a Swap menu. The IIgs starts from the hard disk first, then the 5.25" drives, then the 3.5" ones.

Joystick
Click or press a key to start sound Click the screen to use the mouse.

Left Alt (Option on a Mac) is the Command (Apple) key and Right Alt is Option. Ctrl+F12 is Ctrl-Reset, and with Command held it restarts; Ctrl+Left Alt+Esc opens the Control Panel. Click the screen for the mouse, and Esc to let it go. To play a joystick game on the keyboard, choose keys for the joystick (keys it uses don't type, so set it back to No keys for typing). Gamepads work without taking any keys. The speaker plays; the Ensoniq sound chip doesn't yet.
